---
title: Planning with the Plan tab
description: Find the tasks that still need a day and drag them onto a calendar, on a phone or a computer.
---

The **Plan** tab is where you decide when things happen. It isn't a place for appointments: it
shows your tasks on a calendar by their due date and lets you move them around by dragging. Swiping a task to
**Today**, **Tmrw** or **Next week** handles one task at a time; Plan is for when you have a
handful, or a whole backlog, to spread across the days ahead.

For where this fits in a typical day, see [A daily workflow](/using/workflow/).

## What's on the screen

At the top is a **month grid**. A dot under a day means tasks are due then, colored by the most
important one. Tap a day to select it.

- Swipe the month sideways to move to the next or previous month, or use the arrows at the top.
- Drag the month upwards to shrink it to a single week, which leaves more room for the days below.
  Swiping sideways then moves a week at a time. Drag it down again to bring back the month.
- Tap the month's name at the top to jump back to today.
- On a computer the calendar starts as a single week, so the day columns get the room. Click the
  small arrow under it to open the whole month, and again to close it.

Below the grid, the selected day and the days after it, in one of these views:

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | A scrolling agenda of the next days with tasks, starting from the selected day |
| **2 days**, **3 days** | Side-by-side columns, one per day |
| **Week** | Seven columns, Sunday to Saturday *(on a computer or a wide tablet)* |

The view button switches between them. To look over the next few days without planning, Agenda can
show them too: see [A daily workflow](/using/workflow/). While a column view is showing, the days it
covers are shaded on the month grid, so you can see where you are.

The sort button chooses between **Custom**, where you arrange each day's tasks yourself, and
**Priority**, highest first. **Completed** shows finished tasks too; they're hidden by default.

Adding a task while Plan is open makes it due on the selected day.

## Planning on a phone

1. Tap **Plan task** at the bottom of the screen. A sheet slides up with the tasks that still need
   a day: everything with **no date**, plus everything **overdue**, highest priority first.
2. Narrow it down if the list is long. Search by title, use the filters to pick lists, tags or a
   different due range, or change the sort. Drag the sheet's handle up to see more at once.
3. **Long-press a task and drag it.** The sheet moves aside, and the calendar is yours: drop the task
   on a day in the month grid, or on a day in the agenda or columns below.
4. To move several at once, **tap them first** to select them, then long-press any one of the
   selected tasks. They travel together and land on the same day.

Changed your mind mid-drag? While you're carrying a task, the **Plan task** button turns into
**Drag here to cancel**. Drop it there and nothing changes.

## Planning on a computer

The planning pane sits on the left, with the same search, filters and starting list as the phone's
sheet. The columns button in the header (**Show tasks to plan**) shows or hides it; a wide window
remembers the choice, and a narrower one starts with it hidden. Drag the divider between the pane
and the calendar to make either one wider.

With room to spare, the view, sort and **Completed** controls sit in the header beside the
calendar's name. Clicking a task opens it over the calendar rather than beside it, so the columns
don't change width; press Escape or click outside it to close it.

Drag a task from the pane onto any day. Click several tasks first to drag them together. The
**Week** view is the one to plan in: you can see the whole week's load and drop tasks straight into
the column for each day.

## Moving tasks that already have a day

Everything on the calendar can be dragged, not only what's in the pane:

- **To another day:** drag a task from the agenda or a column onto a different day, in the columns or
  on the month grid. A task with a due time keeps its time.
- **Within a day:** with the sort on **Custom**, drag a task up or down inside its day to change its
  order. Drop it into another day's list and it lands where you let go.

To open a task, tap or click it.

## Tips

- **Start a planning session from the default list.** Undated and overdue is the backlog that
  actually needs decisions. Work down it until it's empty, or until what's left clearly doesn't
  belong on a calendar and should live in a list instead.
- **Use the month grid for the long view.** Dropping onto a day three weeks out is a single drag,
  and the dots show at a glance which days are already busy.
- **Balance the week in the Week view.** If one column is much longer than the rest, drag a few of
  its tasks to a lighter day.
- **Overdue tasks are a signal.** If the same task keeps showing up overdue, give it a realistic day,
  or ask whether it needs doing at all.
