---
title: Reminders
description: Setting reminders, and Mark done and Snooze from the notification.
---

A reminder notifies you about a task before it's due. It needs a due date: open the task, set
its date, then add a reminder for the due date itself or a number of days or weeks before, at the
time you choose. A task can have more than one.

Reminders sync like everything else about a task, so setting one on your phone also sets it on
your other phones.

## How they're delivered

Each phone schedules its own notifications. Nothing goes through a push service, and your server
never sends a notification. Two things follow from that:

- **Open the app on a phone to bring its reminders up to date.** A reminder set on another device
  is scheduled the next time this one syncs.
- **The browser and Windows app don't show reminders.** Use a phone for them.

Each phone keeps its nearest 60 reminders scheduled, and moves on to later ones as those pass.

## From the notification

A reminder has two buttons. Both work with the app closed.

- **Mark done** completes the task. If the phone can't reach the server, it's sent the next time
  the app opens.
- **Snooze 60 min** brings the reminder back in an hour. A snooze applies only to that phone, so
  it doesn't silence the reminder on your other devices.

## On Android

Android can hold a reminder back by a few minutes to save battery, so one set for 9:00 may arrive
at 9:02. On iPhone, reminders arrive on time.
