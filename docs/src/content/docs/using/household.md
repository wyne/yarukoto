---
title: Household
description: Adding people, sharing lists, and signing devices out.
---

A Yarukoto server holds one household. Everyone in it has their own account and their own private
lists, and lists can be shared with everyone. The person who runs the server is the household's
**owner** and its admin.

## Adding a person

Only an admin can add someone.

1. Open **Settings › Household**.
2. Under **Add a device**, choose **New person** and enter their name.
3. Tap **Show a QR for them to scan**, and have them scan it with their phone.

No camera on their device? Have them choose **Sign in with a code** instead, then enter the code it
shows under **Add a device**, with **New person** selected.

To add another device of your own, choose **My device** instead. See [Signing in](/using/signing-in/)
for both ways.

## Shared and private lists

A new list is private: only you see it. To share one, open the list's options and turn on **Share
with household**. Everyone in the household then sees and edits it, and turning it off makes it
private again.

Only the person who made a list can share it, make it private again, or delete it. Your Inbox is
always private.

## Devices

**Settings › Household** lists the devices signed in to your account, with when each was last
seen. Tap **Sign out** on one you no longer use, or have lost. It needs a new code to sign in
again. An admin sees every device in the household and can sign any of them out.

## Removing a person

An admin can tap **Remove** next to someone under **People**. Their devices are signed out and
their private lists are hidden. Nothing is deleted: **Restore** brings everything back. The owner
can't be removed.

To erase an account rather than hide it, the person deletes it themselves. See
[Deleting your account](/using/deleting-your-account/).
